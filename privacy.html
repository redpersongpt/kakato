Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Kakato: High Heels Hammer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your best score is saved on your device using standard iOS storage. It never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No network requests. The game works fully offline.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before a new version ships.
