How to play

  • Tap anywhere to swing the hammer at its current height.
  • Hit the middle of a block to knock it out cleanly (JUST).
  • Hit the top or bottom edge and the block slides out of line. Hit the opposite edge to straighten it.
  • If you knock a block out while another one is crooked, she loses her balance.
  • Swing too high and you hit her foot.

Common questions

No sound? Kakato respects the silent switch. Flip it off and turn the volume up.

Does it need internet? No. The whole game works offline.

Where is my record saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
